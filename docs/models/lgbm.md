## What it learnt

65 folds, refitted every 63 dates; expanding window from 2010-01-11; first test 2011-02-14 (trained on 23,603 rows to 2011-01-05), last test ending 2026-10-15 (trained on 412,283 rows). Embargo 21 dates, purged on each date's latest label end across venues.

Out-of-sample rank IC (Spearman of score vs realised fwd_xs_21d, per date): mean +0.013, std 0.162, IC/std +0.08, positive on 51% of 4,027 test dates. Naive t 5.1 treats the dates as independent; the 21-session labels overlap, so divide by roughly √21 ≈ 4.6 for an honest one (1.1).
